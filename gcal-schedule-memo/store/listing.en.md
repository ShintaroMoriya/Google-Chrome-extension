## Description

Scheduling across time zones should take seconds, not a calculator.

Schedule Assist adds a small, iPhone-style panel to Google Calendar. Click up to three free times, choose where the other person is, and copy a ready-to-send message with every time shown in their time zone, language and format.

WHAT YOU GET
• Pick from your calendar: hover to see exactly what will be added, then click to add it. Works with free time and with existing events, whatever language Google Calendar is in.
• Their time, their format: choose a city (New York, London, Singapore and more) and a format (English 12h, English 24h, Japanese). Times are copied as, for example, "Mon, Aug 31 · 9:00 – 10:00 PM EDT".
• Their day at a glance: ☀ working hours, 🌅 early or late, 🌙 night, plus a +1/−1 badge when the date changes for them. No more 3 a.m. invitations.
• One tap to copy: times only, propose times, ask to reschedule, or suggest a video call.
• Fast: Alt+Shift+S opens the panel and Alt+Shift+C copies. Recent people are one tap away.
• Calm and safe: clicks are only captured while you are picking. Esc always takes you back. Works in light and dark mode.

PRIVACY
Everything stays in your browser. Schedule Assist does not send your calendar or your times anywhere, has no account, no analytics and no remote code. Permissions: storage, and access to calendar.google.com only.
